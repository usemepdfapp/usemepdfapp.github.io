1. Your documents stay on your device

Everything you do with UseMePDF (creating, merging, splitting and compressing PDFs, rotating pages, Word ↔ PDF conversion, document scanning, text recognition/OCR, adding watermarks and page numbers, adding signatures, adding and removing PDF passwords, batch processing) runs on your device. Your files, photos and document contents are not uploaded to our servers or anyone else's; we do not see or analyze their contents. The app has no server of its own and creates no user account.

Files you save in the app are kept in the app's own storage on your device. This storage is excluded from Android backup and device-to-device transfer and is deleted when you uninstall the app. When the "Also save files to phone" setting is on (the default), or when you choose "Save to phone", a copy of the file is written to your phone's Downloads › usemepdf folder. This copy is in your phone's shared storage: it can be seen in the Files app and in other apps you have given file access to, and it is not deleted when you uninstall the app. You can turn this setting off in the app under Settings → Saving. When you share a file, only you send it to the app you choose.

The camera is used only for document scanning and taking photos, started by you, through the system camera/scanner screen. The app does not request storage permission; it only accesses the files you choose.

If you use the saved signature feature, the signature you draw is stored only on your device, in the app's own settings; it is never sent anywhere and is excluded from backup. You can change the signature in the app, and delete it by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Passwords you add to a PDF, or enter to open a password-protected PDF, are used only during that operation; they are not stored and are never sent anywhere.

2. Third-party services

The app uses the following Google services. These services are subject to their own privacy policies.

Google AdMob (ads) and Google User Messaging Platform (consent form) — in the free version only. To serve and measure ads and to prevent abuse, AdMob may collect and share with Google: IP address (to estimate general location), in-app interactions (app launches, ad views/taps), diagnostic information and device identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, your consent is requested for personalized ads; you can change your choice in the app under Settings → Ad privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. In the Pro version no ads are shown and the ad service is not started. Details: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google ML Kit (text recognition and document scanner) — text recognition runs on the device; your images and recognized text are not sent. ML Kit may send Google diagnostic and usage information (device and app information, an installation-specific identifier, performance metrics, the size of the processed data, error codes) to monitor the service's operation and quality; this information is not shared with third parties.

Google Play Billing — purchasing, renewing and cancelling the Pro subscription is handled by Google Play. Your payment details are not passed to us. The app queries Google Play from your device to verify subscription status; purchase information is not sent to any server of ours.

Google Play in-app review — the app may occasionally show Google Play's rating window. The rating and review you give go directly to Google Play; they are not passed to us or to the app.

Data sent by these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

3. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

4. Deleting your data

Because the app has no server of its own, we hold no data about you. You can delete files in the app from within the app or by uninstalling it; copies saved to the Downloads › usemepdf folder can be deleted with your phone's Files app. For data collected by Google, you can use Google's privacy tools: https://myaccount.google.com

5. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page. For questions: usemepdf.destek@gmail.com
